Refund Policy - synthetic demonstration, version 1, 2026-09-24.
Customers can request a refund within 14 calendar days after delivery for damaged or incorrect goods. Support records the order ID and evidence of the problem. A manager must approve or reject the request within 2 working days.
After manager approval, the payment team initiates the refund within 10 working days. Bank processing time is additional and is not guaranteed by this policy. Do not promise a completed refund without payment confirmation.
A failed delivery does not automatically authorize a refund. Perishable goods returned solely because the customer changed their mind are excluded. Defective perishable goods remain eligible for review. Exceptions require a manager decision.
