Supplier SLA - synthetic demonstration, version 1, 2026-09-24.
A supplier must confirm an available order within 15 minutes of receipt. The default preparation and courier handoff deadline is 120 minutes after confirmation. Contract-specific handoff limits stored on an order override this default; seeded orders can have different limits.
A handoff after the applicable deadline is a supplier SLA violation. For an open order, compare the current time with its deadline. Cancellation is reviewed separately. Record timestamps and the reason before assigning responsibility.
An operations manager reviews repeated violations and contacts the supplier. Contract penalties require a separate signed agreement; this policy does not define an automatic monetary penalty. Policy text alone cannot establish how many violations occurred today.
